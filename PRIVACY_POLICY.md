# Privacy Policy for PokerNow Extension

**Last Updated: December 30, 2025**

## Overview

PokerNow Extension ("the Extension") is a browser extension that enhances the PokerNow.club gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

The Extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

- **User Preferences**: Sound settings, AI assistant selection, display mode preferences
- **Custom Audio Files**: Sound files you upload for turn notifications

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

### Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, including:

- Extension installation and uninstallation events
- Feature usage patterns (which features are enabled)
- General interaction metrics

This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individual users.

### Data NOT Collected

We do **NOT** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, address)
- Poker game data, hand histories, or gameplay information
- Financial information or betting amounts
- Browsing history outside of pokernow.club
- Passwords or authentication credentials

## How We Use Information

- **Local Settings**: To remember your preferences between sessions
- **Analytics**: To understand which features are popular and improve the Extension

## Third-Party Services

### AI Assistants

When you enable AI integration (Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT), game state information is sent to your selected AI provider. This communication:

- Only occurs when you explicitly enable the AI feature
- Is sent directly from your browser to the AI service
- Is subject to the respective AI provider's privacy policy:
  - [Google Gemini Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  - [Anthropic Claude Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
  - [OpenAI ChatGPT Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

## Data Security

- All user preferences are stored locally on your device
- No sensitive data is transmitted to our servers
- We do not maintain any databases containing user information

## Your Rights

You can:

- **Clear all stored data**: Uninstall the Extension to remove all locally stored preferences
- **Disable analytics**: Use browser extensions that block Google Analytics
- **Disable AI features**: Turn off AI integration in the Extension settings

## Children's Privacy

This Extension is not intended for users under 18 years of age, consistent with online poker platform requirements.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
